Archetype is built in the open. This page sets out where the platform is headed: what is partly built and being finished, what is planned next, and the research directions being explored. Everything here describes the software, so it holds for any instance.

For what already works, see [What works today](/funders/status/).

## Funding priorities

In order of how directly funding turns into results:

1. **Annotation statistics and quality control** — planned, with a design proposal. The reporting half is cheap; the supervisor review step is separable.
2. **Sustained maintenance** — dependency and security updates, the public instance, and keeping these docs true to the code. Not a feature, but what everything else rests on.
3. **Interoperability** — importing W3C annotations from other tools, and IIIF collections across many manuscripts.
4. **A second corpus** — bringing another collection onto the platform, to prove it beyond the charters it was rebuilt around.
5. **AI-assisted palaeography** — a phased research programme that depends entirely on grant funding.
